# First GPU experiment, 2026-10-25

**Outcome: no evidence of a GPU-specific neuron among the six candidates tested.** This does not establish that no such neuron exists. The model was Qwen3.5-4B Q8_0, running through the project-owned llama-server on Metal.

Command:

```sh
./find_neuron --model qwen-4B --topic gpu --candidates 6 \
  --doses 0.25 0.5 1 --seeds 42 43 --max-tokens 64 --output runs/gpu-first
```

The experiment completed 294 requests and sampled 16467 tokens. Discovery used eight GPU passages and eight matched negative passages, followed by 18 candidate/dose combinations in both directions. Each tuning condition had three prompts and two seeds. The final held-out conditions each had four prompts and two seeds.

All tuning conditions had a GPU keyword hit rate of zero, as did the unsteered baseline. The deterministic tie-break selected L23 N8380 at dose -0.25 (its topic-associated direction is negative) for held-out evaluation. Held-out baseline, both steering directions, and both random-control directions also scored zero. Unrelated-task keyword retention was 1.0 under the selected intervention, versus 0.875 at baseline. Those keyword scores are proxies, not semantic judgments.

Inspection of the tuning text shows that interventions changed outputs toward topics such as quantum computing, optical computing, and at larger doses invented technologies. Changed text alone is not topic-specific causal evidence. No neuron is entered into a concept registry or labeled as a discovered GPU neuron.

The quoted L14 N8949 coordinate was used only to test the hook, not to assert the tweet's behavioral claim.

## Next experiment

Broaden candidate coverage and improve the discovery objective before spending more on free-generation sweeps. Whole-prompt mean activation contrasts can prioritize general hardware or writing-style features. Useful comparisons are token-local activation contrasts, direct GPU-vs-CPU continuation likelihood under steering, and richer matched negatives. A larger confirmation set and human/independent semantic judgments are required before calling a candidate concept-specific. Adding candidates from a second ranking method should be a declared new experiment with fresh held-out prompts, not tuning on this run's held-out results.
